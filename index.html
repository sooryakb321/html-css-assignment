Welcome to NMSM Government College

N.M.S.M Govt. College, Wayanad was established in the year 1981 as a Public Educational Institution. N.M.S.M Govt. College Wayanad is approved by AICTE, and is affiliated with University of Calicut, Calicut. N.M.S.M Govt. College offers admission in the field of Humanities, Computer Applications and Commerce at UG/ PG level.
